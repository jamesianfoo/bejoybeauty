Healthy hair begins at the Root

Our Story

Over more than a decade as a hairstylist, the conversations in my chair have often gone beyond a haircut. Clients have shared their worries about hair fall, dry or sensitive scalps, and the changes they experience after becoming mothers. I’ve listened to their frustrations and seen how personal these concerns can be.

Those conversations inspired BEJOY. I wanted to create gentle, scalp-first haircare that fits into everyday life—care that starts with listening and gives your scalp the attention it deserves.

Healthy Hair Begins at the Root.

ScalpRevivalSeries

Who It’s For

For anyone concerned about hair fall or a dry, sensitive scalp—including new mums noticing postpartum shedding and frequent travellers whose scalp feels unsettled by changes in climate or routine.

Your 2-Step Scalp Routine

Start with the Shampoo to cleanse away oil, buildup and impurities. Follow with the leave-on Tonic to refresh and care for the scalp after washing.

01

Step 1 — Cleanse

Scalp Revival Shampoo

A gentle anti-hair fall shampoo that cleanses while keeping your scalp and hair hydrated.

How to use

Wet the hair and apply the Shampoo directly to the scalp. Massage gently with your fingertips to create a light lather, focusing on the roots and scalp. Rinse thoroughly. If the scalp is very oily, shampoo twice.

Key benefits

  • Hair Fall Care

    A daily duo that helps reduce hair fall, strengthen weak roots and support a healthier-looking scalp. The Shampoo cleanses gently, while the leave-on Tonic refreshes and cares for the scalp after washing.

  • Scalp Balance & Care

    Gentle enough for everyday use on dry and sensitive scalps, so your scalp feels calm, clean and comfortable.

  • Root & Hair Strengthening

    With Biota Orientalis, He Shou Wu and pomegranate, our scalp-first formula supports stronger-looking, fuller-looking hair.

  • Scalp & Hair Hydration

    With panthenol, glycerin and ectoin to help keep the scalp and hair feeling hydrated and comfortable after cleansing.

02

Step 2 — Nourish

Scalp Revival Tonic

A weightless leave-on tonic that refreshes and nourishes the scalp while helping hair look stronger, fuller, and more resilient. Fast-absorbing and residue-free, it extends your ritual beyond wash day.

How to use

Shake well. Apply directly to the scalp on dry or towel-dried hair, section by section. Massage gently and leave in—no rinsing required. Use morning and night, including between washes.

Key benefits

  • Root Renewal

    Biota Orientalis Leaf Extract and caffeine invigorate the scalp and support stronger-looking hair from the roots.

  • Scalp Harmony

    Pomegranate extract and rice ferment filtrate help maintain a balanced, comfortable, conditioned scalp.

  • Weightless Nourishment

    A fast-absorbing formula nourishes daily without weighing down hair or leaving a greasy finish.

  • Continuous Scalp Care

    A leave-on treatment that makes consistent scalp care effortless, even between washes.
